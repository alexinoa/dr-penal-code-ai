# Citation Standards

## Statutes

Preferred format:

**Fuente:** Ley núm. 74-25, art. 123, párr. I, modificado por Ley núm. 44-26, art. 8.

English:

**Source:** Law No. 74-25, Art. 123, para. I, as amended by Law No. 44-26, Art. 8.

Include numerals or literals when relevant.

## Constitution

**Fuente:** Constitución de la República Dominicana, art. 40, numeral 1.

## Code of Criminal Procedure

**Fuente:** Ley núm. 97-25, Código Procesal Penal, art. 96, numeral 6.

## Jurisprudence

When verified from an official decision, identify:
- court;
- decision number;
- date if available;
- relevant holding or paragraph when available.

Example:

**Jurisprudencia:** Tribunal Constitucional, Sentencia TC/XXXX/YY, [fecha], párr. [número].

Never invent a decision number or paragraph.

## Quotations

If the user asks for exact statutory language:
- quote only verified text;
- identify the source immediately;
- distinguish quotation from paraphrase;
- if the available Markdown appears corrupted by OCR, say so and prefer the official publication.

## Multiple authorities

List the controlling authority first, then supporting authorities.

## Bilingual answers

Do not translate the citation itself into a fictitious official English title. It is acceptable to render `Ley núm.` as `Law No.` while preserving the legal source identity.
