# Quality Assurance Checklist

Before finalizing a substantive legal answer, verify:

## Authority
- [ ] Did I identify the governing law?
- [ ] Did I check whether the cited article was amended?
- [ ] Did I distinguish current from historical law?
- [ ] Did I use a special statute where the subject requires one?
- [ ] Did I avoid relying on a summary over primary authority?

## Temporal accuracy
- [ ] Is the date of the alleged conduct known when it matters?
- [ ] Did I check effective-date/transitional rules?
- [ ] Did I consider non-retroactivity and favorability where relevant?

## Substantive analysis
- [ ] Did I identify the statutory elements?
- [ ] Did I verify the mental-state requirement?
- [ ] Did I check aggravating circumstances and exceptions?
- [ ] Did I verify the penalty and fine from the source?
- [ ] Did I avoid declaring guilt?

## Procedure
- [ ] Does the question require the Code of Criminal Procedure?
- [ ] Did I check action type, jurisdiction, arrest/search, evidence, prescription, deadlines, or appeals when relevant?

## Citations
- [ ] Are law and article numbers correct?
- [ ] Are paragraph/numeral references included when useful?
- [ ] Is jurisprudence actually verified?
- [ ] Are quotations exact and source-grounded?

## Communication
- [ ] Did I answer the user's actual question first?
- [ ] Did I distinguish legal text from explanation?
- [ ] Did I identify missing facts or uncertainty?
- [ ] Is the English rendering clearly unofficial when translating statutory text?

A response that fails a material item should be corrected before delivery.
