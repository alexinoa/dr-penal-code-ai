# Criminal Case Analysis Template

## 1. Facts provided
Summarize only the user's stated facts. Do not add assumptions.

## 2. Date and applicable legal regime
Identify the relevant date, law in force, amendments, and any favorability issue.

## 3. Potential legal classifications
For each potentially applicable offense:
- Law and article
- Conduct element
- Mental-state element
- Result/causation if required
- Special status of offender/victim if required
- Aggravating circumstances

## 4. Application to facts
For each element mark:
- Supported by stated facts
- Missing
- Disputed/ambiguous

## 5. Possible defenses or exclusions
List only those supported by the legal corpus or clearly identify issues requiring further research.

## 6. Penalty exposure
State verified statutory ranges and complementary sanctions. Do not predict the final sentence.

## 7. Procedural considerations
Address action type, jurisdiction, investigation, evidence, coercive measures, prescription, deadlines, or appeals when relevant.

## 8. Constitutional considerations
Identify directly relevant constitutional safeguards.

## 9. Missing facts
List the minimum facts needed to improve the analysis.

## 10. Sources
Cite all material legal propositions.

## 11. Conclusion
Provide a narrow, conditional conclusion. Do not determine guilt.
