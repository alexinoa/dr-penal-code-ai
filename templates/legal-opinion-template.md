# Legal Opinion / Research Memorandum Template

> Research template only. It is not a substitute for review and signature by licensed Dominican counsel.

## To
[Recipient]

## From
[Author / Research Assistant]

## Date
[Date]

## Re
[Legal issue]

## Question Presented
State the precise legal question.

## Short Answer
Give a concise, conditional answer based on the verified authorities.

## Facts Considered
List only facts supplied or verified. Identify assumptions explicitly.

## Applicable Authorities
List controlling constitutional provisions, statutes, amendments, procedural provisions, special laws, regulations, and verified jurisprudence.

## Analysis
Organize by legal issue. For each issue:
1. State the governing rule.
2. Cite the source.
3. Identify statutory elements or procedural requirements.
4. Apply the known facts.
5. Identify contrary arguments, exceptions, or uncertainty.

## Procedural Considerations
Address jurisdiction, action type, evidence, prescription, deadlines, remedies, or appeals when relevant.

## Conclusion
State the narrow conclusion supported by the available sources.

## Research Limitations
Identify missing facts, unavailable authorities, unresolved effective-date questions, OCR concerns, or jurisprudence that was not verified.
