# DR Penal Code AI — Master Reference Index

This file consolidates the retrieval aids that would otherwise consume multiple Custom GPT Knowledge slots. It is an index and navigation aid only. Official legal texts always control.

## 1. Core source map

- Constitution of the Dominican Republic: fundamental rights, due process, legality, privacy, liberty, property, equality, judicial protection, public administration.
- Ley 74-25: current Penal Code baseline.
- Ley 44-26: amendments to Ley 74-25; check first whenever an article may have been modified.
- Ley 97-25: current Code of Criminal Procedure.
- Prior Penal Code: historical and transitional analysis only.

## 2. Special-law map

- Cybercrime / unlawful access / digital systems / interception / electronic fraud: Ley 53-07.
- Money laundering / terrorist financing / predicate offenses / preventive obligations: Ley 155-17.
- Firearms / ammunition / explosives / licensing / unlawful possession or trafficking: Ley 631-16.
- Drugs / controlled substances / possession / distribution / trafficking: Ley 50-88.
- Children and adolescents / juvenile justice / child protection / child victims: Ley 136-03.
- Human trafficking / migrant smuggling: Ley 137-03.
- Migration status / entry / stay / deportation / migration offenses: Ley 285-04.
- Ministerio Público / prosecution authority / investigation direction: Ley 133-11.
- Policía Nacional / police powers / duties / investigations / detention-related authority: Ley 590-16.
- Public asset declarations / false declarations / illicit enrichment issues: Ley 311-14.

## 3. Topic-to-source cross-reference

### Arrest, detention, questioning, coercive measures
Check Constitution + Ley 97-25 + Ley 590-16 + Ley 133-11.

### Search, seizure, interception, digital evidence
Check Constitution + Ley 97-25 + Ley 53-07 + any sector-specific law.

### Homicide, bodily harm, sexual offenses, property offenses, fraud, public corruption
Check Ley 74-25, then Ley 44-26, then relevant special law and Ley 97-25.

### Domestic or intrafamily violence
Check Ley 74-25 as amended by Ley 44-26, child-protection rules if minors are involved, and Ley 97-25 for procedure.

### Cyber harassment or online conduct
Check Ley 74-25 as amended by Ley 44-26 and Ley 53-07.

### Drug offenses
Check Ley 50-88 first for drug-specific classifications and thresholds, then current Penal Code principles and Ley 97-25.

### Firearms
Check Ley 631-16 plus any offense under Ley 74-25 and procedural rules under Ley 97-25.

### Money laundering
Check Ley 155-17, the alleged predicate offense, Ley 97-25, and constitutional safeguards.

### Public corruption / illicit enrichment
Check Ley 74-25, Ley 44-26, Ley 311-14, Ley 155-17 where laundering is implicated, and Ley 97-25.

### Human trafficking / migrant smuggling
Check Ley 137-03, Ley 285-04, Ley 74-25 as amended, child-protection law where relevant, and Ley 97-25.

### Minors
Check Ley 136-03 in addition to the substantive offense law and Constitution. Do not apply adult criminal procedure automatically to juvenile cases.

## 4. Constitutional-rights triggers

When facts implicate any of the following, consult the current Constitution directly:

- legality and non-retroactivity;
- equality and non-discrimination;
- personal liberty;
- integrity and dignity;
- privacy and intimacy;
- inviolability of home or communications;
- property;
- freedom of expression;
- due process;
- presumption of innocence;
- right to defense;
- judicial protection;
- rights of children and adolescents;
- limits on public authority.

Do not cite constitutional article numbers from this index unless verified in the Constitution file.

## 5. Crime-analysis index

For any alleged offense locate and verify:

1. exact statutory provision;
2. prohibited act or omission;
3. required result;
4. causation;
5. mental state;
6. protected victim or offender status;
7. attempt;
8. participation/complicity;
9. corporate liability if relevant;
10. aggravating circumstances;
11. statutory exceptions or excuses;
12. penalty range;
13. fine basis;
14. complementary penalties;
15. prescription;
16. related special laws;
17. procedural consequences.

## 6. Penalty-retrieval rule

Never rely on this index as a penalty table. Penalties must be retrieved from the controlling statute and any amendment. If a penalty appears in an older law, verify whether it remains current before using it.

## 7. Bilingual terminology guide

Prefer concept-preserving translations rather than U.S. common-law substitutions.

- imputado: accused person / defendant, depending on procedural stage
- querella: criminal complaint / private complaint, depending on context
- Ministerio Público: Public Prosecutor's Office / prosecution service
- medida de coerción: coercive measure / pretrial measure
- prisión preventiva: pretrial detention
- prescripción: limitation period / statute of limitations, depending on context
- tentativa: attempt
- autor: principal offender / perpetrator
- cómplice: accomplice
- agravante: aggravating circumstance
- atenuante: mitigating circumstance
- eximente: exonerating circumstance / defense, depending on context
- sobreseimiento: dismissal / discontinuance, depending on procedural posture
- sentencia firme: final judgment
- cosa irrevocablemente juzgada: final res judicata / final adjudication
- allanamiento: search of premises / search operation, depending on context
- incautación: seizure
- decomiso: forfeiture / confiscation, depending on context

When an English term could distort Dominican law, keep the Spanish term in parentheses.

## 8. Old-versus-new law index

When conduct may predate the current Penal Code:

- identify the date of conduct;
- locate the prior Penal Code provision;
- locate the corresponding Ley 74-25 provision;
- check Ley 44-26;
- compare penalties and legal elements;
- analyze non-retroactivity and favorability;
- clearly label historical versus current law.

## 9. Jurisprudence retrieval rule

If official jurisprudence is uploaded, identify court, decision number, date, legal issue, holding, and relevant paragraph when available. Never infer a precedent from summaries alone and never create a case citation from memory.

Search terms are retrieval aids only; the exact statutory label controls.

## 11. Final retrieval check

Before relying on a result confirm:

- the document is the intended law;
- the article number is verified;
- the version is current for the relevant date;
- later amendments were checked;
- OCR or conversion errors are not affecting the text;
- the answer distinguishes statute, procedure, jurisprudence, and secondary guidance.
