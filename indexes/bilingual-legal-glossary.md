# Bilingual Legal Glossary

This glossary supports explanation and retrieval. It does not create official English translations.

| Spanish term | Informal English equivalent | Note |
|---|---|---|
| imputado | accused person / defendant | Context matters; preserve `imputado` when precision is important. |
| querellante | private complainant / private accuser | Dominican procedural role; avoid forcing a U.S. equivalent. |
| actor civil | civil claimant in the criminal proceeding | Party seeking civil reparation within the criminal case. |
| Ministerio Público | Public Prosecution Service / Public Prosecutor's Office | Preserve institutional name when useful. |
| medida de coerción | coercive measure / pretrial measure | Includes legally authorized measures restricting liberty or other rights. |
| prisión preventiva | pretrial detention | Not a sentence. |
| presunción de inocencia | presumption of innocence | Fundamental procedural guarantee. |
| no autoincriminación | privilege against self-incrimination | Right not to be compelled to incriminate oneself. |
| acción pública | public criminal action | Prosecuted by the Ministerio Público subject to applicable rules. |
| acción privada | private criminal action | Prosecuted by the victim/authorized private party under the procedural code. |
| instancia privada | private complaint/request required to activate prosecution | Do not confuse with fully private criminal action. |
| tentativa | attempt | Incomplete commission where legally punishable. |
| autor | principal offender / perpetrator | Translation depends on statutory context. |
| cómplice | accomplice | Preserve statutory meaning from the applicable law. |
| agravante | aggravating circumstance | May increase classification or penalty. |
| atenuante | mitigating circumstance | Use only when the legal source recognizes it. |
| prescripción | limitation period / statute of limitations | Context may be action, penalty, or another limitation regime. |
| extinción de la acción penal | extinguishment of criminal action | Procedural consequence ending prosecution where legally applicable. |
| decomiso | forfeiture / confiscation | Exact English term depends on context. |
| bienes jurídicos protegidos | legally protected interests | Criminal-law concept; not simply physical property. |
| dolo | intent / criminal intent | Do not translate automatically as malice. |
| culpa | negligence / fault | Exact meaning depends on the statute. |

## Translation rule

When an English common-law term could distort a Dominican civil-law concept, retain the Spanish term in parentheses and explain it rather than pretending there is a perfect equivalent.
