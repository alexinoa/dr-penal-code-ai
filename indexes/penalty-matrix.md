## Maintenance rule

For every future row:
1. Verify the exact article in the official text.
2. Check whether the offense or penalty was amended or superseded.
3. Record fines exactly as the statute defines the salary/reference base.
4. Record aggravated variants separately.
5. Never use this matrix as the sole authority in a user answer; re-check the statute.
